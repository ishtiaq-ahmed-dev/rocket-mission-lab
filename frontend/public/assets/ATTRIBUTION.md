# Asset sources

Downloaded 19 October 2026 from Poly Haven. These assets are CC0 and are bundled locally so the page does not require third-party network calls at runtime.

- **Rocky Terrain 02**, Amal Kumar. Diffuse, OpenGL normal and roughness maps (1K derivatives used by the page). [Source](https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_terrain_02).
- [Poly Haven CC0 asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license).

Rocket geometry, panel/soot/rivet texture, landing pad markings, road surface and ocean shading are generated by this project. The rocket is a generic educational booster, not an official vehicle model.
