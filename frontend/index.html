MODEL NOTES · APPROXIMATE SIMULATION

Independent educational model, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or derived from SpaceX flight data. Every figure here is simulated, not a vehicle specification.

VEHICLE

  • Generic Falcon 9 Block 5-class masses; Merlin 1D at 845 kN sea level / 914 kN vacuum; MVac at 934 kN.
  • Point-mass dynamics with Mach-dependent drag and an angle-of-attack normal force, a 1976 standard atmosphere and steady wind.

GUIDANCE

  • Gravity-turn ascent, main engine cutoff on propellant reserve, 3 s stage separation, predictor-corrector boostback, entry burn, grid-fin steering and a closed-loop landing burn.
  • The second stage follows a tuned pitch schedule to a 300 km insertion.

NOT MODELLED

  • 6-DOF rigid-body dynamics, gimbal and RCS actuator limits, propellant slosh, aeroelasticity, sensor noise, abort modes, weather and range safety.
  • Plume, soot, grid-fin and leg motion are illustrative.
